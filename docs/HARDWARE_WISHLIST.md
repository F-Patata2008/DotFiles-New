### 1. Daily Driver Smartphone: Samsung Galaxy A37 vs. Nothing Phone (4a)
* **Target Role:** Primary mobile field terminal, secure communications, and daily commuter driver.
* **Why Desired:**
  - Absolute rejection of the disposable Chinese budget philosophy (e.g., Poco F3 motherboard solder failures, Redmi 2-year obsolescence with 100W battery-cooking chargers).
  - Absolute rejection of boring $900+ bourgeois iPhones with identical hardware iterations from iPhone 11 to 17.
  - **Samsung Galaxy A37 candidate:** 
    - Proven multi-year Android OS + security update commitment (4 to 5+ years of software support).
    - IP67 water/dust resistance (crucial for outdoor rain and gym/pool bag resilience).
    - Dependable AMOLED 120Hz display with full Chilean Sub-6 5G (n28 / n78) and SUBTEL SAE emergency homologation.
  - **Nothing Phone (4a) candidate:**
    - Clean Nothing OS without intrusive bloatware or predatory telemetry.
    - Transparent retro-futuristic Glyph aesthetic with practical countdown/notification glyphs.
    - Balanced midranger silicon with open bootloader friendliness.

### 4. Sofle Split Ergonomic Keyboard
* **Target Architecture:** 58-key split ortholinear/column-staggered layout.
* **Firmware:** QMK / VIAL running on dual RP2040-Zero microcontrollers (133 MHz dual ARM Cortex-M0+).
* **Key Features:**
  - Dual 128x32 / 128x64 SSD1306 OLED displays (Char Aznable animation + WPM + Layer state).
  - Dual EC11 rotary encoders (volume scrubbing, Neovim code scroll, terminal tabs).
  - Per-key addressable SK6812 MINI-E RGB LEDs.
  - Hotswap sockets (Kailh MX or Choc v1).
* **Acquisition Playbook:**
  - Order 5x reversible PCBs directly from JLCPCB (~$22 USD with shipping).
  - FabLab Beauchef: 3D-print plates and bottom shell on Bambu Lab (zero cost).
  - Laser-cut 3mm acrylic underglow diffuser.
  - Solder SMD LEDs and diodes at uni lab using Hakko regulated irons at 270°C.
  - Sell 2nd PCB pair to a Beauchef classmate to cut net cost down to ~$50,000 CLP.
* **Technical Spec:** [`docs/SOFLE_SPLIT_KEYBOARD_SPEC.md`](SOFLE_SPLIT_KEYBOARD_SPEC.md)

### 6. Dual "Trash Can" Mac Pro Homelab Cluster (MacPro6,1)
* **Target Role:** 24/7 subterranean home server infrastructure, hypervisor, and private storage cluster.
* **The Hardware Architecture:**
  - **Form Factor:** The legendary late-2013 Apple Mac Pro cylindrical "Trash Can" (*MacPro6,1*).
  - **CPU:** Intel Xeon E5 v2 (up to 12-core / 24-thread per machine).
  - **Memory:** 4-channel ECC DDR3 (expandable up to 64GB or 128GB ECC RAM per node).
  - **Thermal Design:** Unified central triangular heatsink core with a single ultra-quiet top exhaust blower fan.
  - **I/O Arsenal:** Dual Gigabit Ethernet ports, 4x USB 3.0, 6x Thunderbolt 2 (for external PCIe expansion / high-speed storage backplanes), and internal NVMe PCIe SSD.
* **Why "Trash Can" Macs over Generic 1L Mini PCs?**
  - **Proletarian Repurposing of Bourgeois Relics:** Abandoned by Apple in modern macOS updates, these machines sell for fractions of their original $4,000+ price tag on the used market.
  - **ECC Memory & Multi-Thread Muscle:** 24 combined Xeon cores and up to 128GB–256GB ECC RAM across two cylindrical nodes provide server-grade reliability for pennies on the dollar.
  - **Clustered High Availability:** Running **Proxmox VE (PVE)** or **Debian Headless** across both nodes with dual Gigabit interconnects for Jellyfin transcoding, local Git mirrors, containerized simulations, and automated backups.
  - **Aesthetic Dominance:** Two matte-black aluminum cylinders sitting on the homelab shelf look like twin fusion reactors.
